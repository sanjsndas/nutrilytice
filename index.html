Four formats of personalized diet planning

To address our clients' diverse needs Nutrilyticetae offers four targeted services for creating personalized nutrition systems:

  • Expert development of a customized menu. We create a detailed nutrition program with a precise distribution of proteins, fats, carbohydrates, and micronutrients tailored to your goals, from weight management to muscle gain.
  • Nutrigenetic and analytical audit. In-depth analysis of laboratory tests, assessment of current vitamin and mineral status, and consideration of reactions to specific food groups to prevent deficiencies.
  • Sports and functional modeling. Designing highly specialized diets for individuals with increased physical and mental stress, aimed at effectively maintaining endurance.
  • Full personalized supervision. Weekly adjustments to the plan, monitoring of progress, emotional support, and regular consultations throughout all stages of the new regimen.

Our analytical and personal approach

Nutrilyticetae is based on a step-by-step system of collecting and processing information.
